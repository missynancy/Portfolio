Project overview

Moving from an equipment list to an operating model.

An anonymised client was exploring a B2B rental fleet designed to support catering for up to 400 people. The brief called for more than a list of items to buy: it needed a structured view of what assets were required, what they could cost, how rentals could be managed and how activity could be recorded.

My role: I developed the research, organised the asset and costing information, compared sourcing approaches, and translated the proposal into an operational and digital tracking model.

The challenge

Six practical questions behind the proposal.

  1. 01What should be purchased?
  2. 02How much would it cost?
  3. 03How could the assets generate rental income?
  4. 04How would the assets be operated?
  5. 05How would inventory be tracked?
  6. 06How would partner activity and payouts be recorded?

Digital systems

Digital accountability for physical assets.

The recommended Airtable approach gives each asset a unique identity, then connects it to the operational events around it. This is digital operations and systems thinking: making the work traceable, reviewable and easier to manage remotely.

Physical Asset→Unique ID→Inventory Record→Booking→Partner Activity→Return→Payout
